Harp

“Praise him with the sounding of the trumpet, praise him with the harp and lyre.” Harps belong in worship — the psalms are full of them. Tap a string, or draw your finger across them all.

Tap a string to pluck it · press and drag across the strings for a glissando

Like a real harp, the C strings are red and the F strings are dark blue, so your fingers can find their place; the rest are natural gut color. This one is tuned in C major, a lever harp's home key, from C3 up to C6.

Every sound is synthesized live on your device with the Web Audio API — no recordings, no samples, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
